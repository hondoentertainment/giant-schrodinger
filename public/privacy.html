Venn with Friends · Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 12, 2026

Venn with Friends is a creative word game from Hondo Entertainment. This page is the public privacy policy for the web app (giant-schrodinger.vercel.app) and the optional iOS wrapper that loads the same web experience.

What we store on your device

Gameplay progress lives locally in your browser or WKWebView storage: profile, gallery, achievements, mute preference, and settings. The iOS app does not add a separate account database.

What we store on our servers

When you enable Supabase-backed features, shared rounds, judgements, multiplayer rooms, and content reports may be stored in our database. Custom uploads go to Supabase Storage when backend services are enabled. Do not upload content you do not have rights to share.

Third-party services

Optional telemetry (Sentry, PostHog) helps us monitor crashes and product usage when those keys are configured. AI scoring may send your submission text and prompt labels to Google Gemini through a server-side edge function. Media lookups may call Pexels and Giphy. YouTube embeds may load when a round uses video prompts.

Children

The game is rated for a general audience (4+ / Everyone). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Do not enter a real email or full name if you are creating a casual play profile.

Retention and deletion

Local data can be cleared by resetting site data in the browser or deleting the iOS app. For hosted multiplayer or friend-judge records, email support@hondoentertainment.com and we will delete associated rows we can identify.
